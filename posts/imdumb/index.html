And then?

The point 🤝 Me not getting it
Written June 16, 2026 · Modified June 16, 2026 · By Omar
I’m dumb. I don’t get things.

If the so called AI is unlocking incredible productivity gains (as the linkedin weirdos like to say), why is everyone still working so much all the time? If we go further and listen to the psycho CEO’s when they say that our jobs are going to be automated away altogether by these computer programs, what are we to imagine what that next step of the world looks like? Who will buy the things that the economic vampires are trying to sell? If all of this is so obvious, why is my MP (Evan Solomon, Minister of so called AI) acting like he’s the Chief Marketing Officer of every AI company and not working closely with the Minister of Jobs and Families to put together a plan to protect the people of our communities when this oh-so-scary future happens?

And if it is so amazing and smart, why is everyone always trying to scare me by telling me I’m going to be left behind? I’ll be able to pick this shit up whenever I want and get as productive as any of the dumb fucks on linkedin claim they are, so what’s the problem?

Speaking of the Minister of Jobs and Families (Patty Hajdu), why did she try to end the Air Canada Flight Attendants’ strike? Presumably her job is to protect Canadian workers and their families, but I think she thinks she’s the CEO of CEOs or something. She ordered the workers to return to work, giving the corporation exactly what they wanted. The Union decided to say fuck it; they broke the law and continued the strike and the corporation folded almost immediately. Their entire plan hinged on the fact that the minister in charge of giving a shit about workers would toe the line and tell them to get fucked. Why did the person who was supposed to help the people try to fuck them?

I don’t get that.

What is the point of going to Mars to colonize it if we can’t even agree to take care of the planet we live on? Imagine that we had a magic telescope through which we could see millions of light years away. Imagine we found a beautiful blue-green planet with white clouds, temperate, habitable zones, and flora and fauna that lived on it. We’d be awestruck that we found something so incredibly rare and so fantastic in the cold dark of space.

We literally have that, we live on it. Instead of protecting it and acting like we even give a shit, we are hell-bent on extracting every last drop of everything useful to us, killing entire species as we do. Is it just corporations and rich fucks pretending that “spreading the light of consciousness” is important to them so that they continue to get investment? Surely if they gave a damn about the light of consciousness they’d try (or even pretend to try) to take care of our amazing, beautiful world. To uplift the people that are currently alive here, now. To protect and revitalize the natural world.

I think that I might be dumb.

The fucking “Hydration Break” in this current world cup.

What the fuck is the deal with Nationalism? I’d love to see the overlap between the people that don’t know or care about their neighbours, the people that don’t participate or contribute to their communities, and the people that want to rub the national flag all over their bodies and scream-sing the national anthem. I bet it’s just a circle.

I don’t care about that stuff, but I love the place I live in and I care about the people in my community. It’s like that’s not enough for some people. You have to be proud to be a Canadian and you have to show it in very specific ways.

Brother, I’m not even proud to be myself, so shove it up your ass.
